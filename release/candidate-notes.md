Folder Camera 1.0.0 release candidate

Native Android folder camera with local-first JPEG capture, searchable nested Unicode paths, photo previews/share/export, zoom and continuous light, verified local-save recovery and optional secure LAN copies.

The Linux receiver automatically selects its private LAN address, stores preferences and displays its first pairing QR. It remains optional.

This is a public release candidate for F-Droid reproducible-build validation and store preparation. Google Play and official F-Droid publication are not live yet. The SARL Play account, final price and company support contacts remain owner setup items. Android 16 native acceptance passed; Android 17/16-KB emulator validation is being completed. Use generated/test photos while evaluating the candidate.

Production ID: io.github.sigmasd.foldercamera. This is separate from the earlier prototype org.foldercamera; recover private staging before removing the prototype. Public folder photos remain intact. The candidate APK uses the developer-controlled production signing certificate.
